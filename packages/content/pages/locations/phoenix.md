# Phoenix Office

Harbor & Vale Law maintains its principal office in downtown Phoenix, convenient to the Maricopa County Superior Court and regional transit hubs.

## Address and parking

Our Phoenix office is located at 400 W Washington St, Suite 1200, Phoenix, AZ 85003. Visitor parking is accessible directly beneath the building structure, with parking validation provided to clients attending scheduled in-person meetings. The building is fully accessible and situated within walking distance of the central light rail line.

## Hours

Our Phoenix office is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM for scheduled appointments. Attorneys are available for emergency consultations outside regular hours by prior arrangement with our intake team.
