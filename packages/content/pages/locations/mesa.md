# Mesa Office

Harbor & Vale Law operates an East Valley branch office in historic downtown Mesa, serving clients throughout Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Tempe, and Apache Junction.

## Address and parking

Our Mesa office is situated at 55 N Center St, Suite 300, Mesa, AZ 85201. Dedicated surface parking is located directly adjacent to the main building entrance, with complimentary client parking stalls clearly marked. The location is easily accessible from both the US-60 and Loop 202 freeways.

## Hours

Our Mesa location is open for client consultations Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM by appointment. Client meetings can be scheduled at this facility to provide convenient access without traveling to our central Phoenix location.
