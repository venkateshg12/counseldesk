# Privacy Policy and Website Disclaimers

Harbor & Vale Law values your privacy and is committed to protecting information submitted through our website and interactive online tools.

## What we collect

When you interact with our website, request a consultation, or communicate with our intake team, we collect contact information such as your name, telephone number, email address, and general background details describing your legal inquiry. We use this information solely to evaluate potential representation, respond to your inquiries, and schedule consultations.

## How the website assistant works

Our website features an interactive automated assistant designed to help visitors explore our firm's practice areas, review office locations, understand fee structures, and initiate consultation requests. Conversations with the website assistant are processed securely to provide relevant informational responses and assist our staff in scheduling appointments.

## Not legal advice

The information provided on this website and by our interactive website assistant is for general educational and informational purposes only and does not constitute formal legal advice. Viewing this website or communicating with our website assistant does not create an attorney-client relationship. An attorney-client relationship is formed only after both parties execute a formal written legal representation agreement.
