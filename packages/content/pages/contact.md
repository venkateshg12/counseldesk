# Contact Us

Connect with Harbor & Vale Law to schedule an appointment, request case evaluation, or inquire about legal representation in Arizona.

## Call or email

To reach our team directly, call our main telephone line at (602) 555-0142, or send correspondence to contact@harborvalelaw.com. Prospective clients can also submit an inquiry through our website assistant at any time of day to request a prompt callback from our intake team.

## Office hours

Our office hours are Monday through Friday from 8:00 to 18:00, Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00, and closed Sunday. If you require immediate legal attention regarding an arrest or detention outside our operating hours, leave a message on our emergency extension for an on-call response.

## Languages

We are committed to serving Arizona's diverse population. Our legal consultations are available in English, and consultations also in Spanish can be arranged upon request. Bilingual staff members assist throughout initial intake interviews and ongoing case communications.
