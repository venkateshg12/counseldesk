# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our legal practice, representation scope, and client communications at Harbor & Vale Law.

## General questions

Prospective clients frequently ask how soon we can begin working on their case. Once you sign our representation agreement and complete initial retainer or fee arrangements, our legal team begins immediate investigation, requests discovery, and files required notices of appearance with the appropriate court.

## Cases we don't handle

We do not handle immigration, bankruptcy, tax, or patent matters. If you need help with one of these, we are happy to refer you to another firm. By focusing strictly on our primary practice areas, we ensure our attorneys maintain deep knowledge and practical courtroom experience in their fields.

## Working with us

Throughout your legal matter, you maintain regular contact with your assigned attorney and our dedicated paralegal staff. We provide case updates by phone, secure email, and scheduled conferences. We explain each legal development clearly so you remain informed and confident in the decisions made regarding your representation.
