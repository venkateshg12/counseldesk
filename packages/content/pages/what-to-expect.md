# What to Expect When Working With Us

Retaining legal counsel is an important commitment. We want every client to know what to expect from the moment they contact our firm until their matter concludes.

## Your first meeting

During your initial consultation, we focus on understanding your circumstances and objectives. Your attorney will review available documents, explain the legal framework governing your issue, outline realistic courses of action, and answer questions regarding fees and procedures. We ensure you leave with a clear roadmap of prospective next steps.

## Communication

Transparent and prompt communication is central to our practice. We keep you apprised of all meaningful developments, including court dates, opposing counsel communications, and procedural deadlines. You will have direct telephone and secure email access to your legal team, and our staff aims to return non-urgent messages within one business day.

## Timelines

Legal proceedings often involve court schedules, statutory waiting periods, and procedural discovery that take time to resolve. While litigation timelines depend on court calendars and opposing party responsiveness, we work continuously to advance your case efficiently and prevent avoidable delays at every stage of the representation.
