## How we charge

We explain our fee before any work starts, in writing. How we charge depends on the type of case.

### Flat fees

Most criminal and DUI cases are handled for a flat fee. A flat fee covers the work described in your agreement, however many hours it takes, so the price does not grow as the case goes on.

### Contingency fees

Personal injury cases are handled on contingency. You pay no fee unless we recover money for you.

### Hourly work

Family law and estate planning matters are usually billed by the hour. We give you an estimate at the start and update it if the case changes.

## DUI fees

Representation for a first-offense misdemeanor DUI starts at a flat fee of $2,500. Extreme DUI and felony DUI cases are quoted after a free consultation, because the work involved varies widely.

## Personal injury fees

Our contingency fee is 33% of the recovery if your case settles before a lawsuit is filed, and 40% if a lawsuit is filed. Case costs are explained in your agreement.

## Payment plans

For criminal and DUI cases we offer monthly payment plans. Ask about them during your free consultation.
