# Personal Injury in Arizona

When an unforeseen accident results in serious physical harm due to someone else's negligence, navigating medical treatment and insurance bureaucracy can feel overwhelming.

## Cases we take

Harbor & Vale Law represents injured individuals across Arizona involved in passenger car accidents, commercial trucking collisions, motorcycle crashes, bicycle and pedestrian incidents, and premises liability claims involving slip-and-fall hazards on commercial property. We handle matters involving catastrophic bodily harm, traumatic brain trauma, spinal damage, and complex orthopedic injuries.

## What happens after an accident

Following an accident, prompt action is critical to preserve vital evidence and establish your claim. We recommend securing immediate medical evaluation, photographing accident scenes and vehicle damage, and collecting witness contact information. Our legal team promptly communicates with insurance carriers, gathers police investigation reports, and handles adjuster inquiries so you can focus on physical recovery.

## How compensation works

Under Arizona personal injury law, an injured party may pursue compensatory damages for economic and non-economic losses caused by a negligent party. Recoverable damages often include past and anticipated future medical expenses, lost wages, reduced future earning capacity, physical pain, and loss of life enjoyment. We assemble comprehensive settlement demand packages backed by medical and financial records.
