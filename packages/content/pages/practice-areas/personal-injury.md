# Personal Injury in Arizona

When an accident that was someone else's fault leaves you badly hurt, the medical care and insurance paperwork can feel like too much. We help you with both.

## Cases we take

We represent people hurt in Arizona in car accidents, truck crashes, motorcycle crashes, bicycle and pedestrian accidents, and slip-and-fall accidents on business property. We also handle the most serious injuries, such as brain injuries, spinal cord damage and complex broken bones.

## What happens after an accident

Acting fast helps protect your case. We recommend that you see a doctor right away, take photos of the accident scene and the damage to your vehicle, and write down the names and phone numbers of any witnesses. Our team then talks to the insurance companies, collects the police report and deals with the adjusters, so you can focus on getting better.

## How compensation works

Arizona law lets you ask for money from the person at fault, called damages, for what the accident cost you. That includes money you lost, such as medical bills (now and in the future), lost pay and lower earning power later. It also includes things that are hard to put a price on, such as pain and no longer being able to enjoy life as before. We put together a full settlement demand backed by your medical and financial records.
