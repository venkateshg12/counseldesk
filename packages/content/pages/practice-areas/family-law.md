# Family Law in Arizona

Family legal disputes involve deeply personal transitions that require both steady legal guidance and practical problem-solving. Harbor & Vale Law helps clients protect their children and assets.

## Divorce

We guide spouses through contested and uncontested dissolution of marriage proceedings across Arizona. Arizona is a no-fault divorce jurisdiction, meaning neither party must prove marital misconduct. Our attorneys assist clients with the identification and equitable division of community property, allocation of marital liabilities, determination of separate property claims, and claims for spousal maintenance support.

## Child custody

In Arizona, child custody is legally categorized into legal decision-making authority and parenting time schedules. Courts decide custody based on the best interests of the minor children, assessing parental involvement, stability, and emotional bonds. We help parents formulate detailed, sustainable parenting plans that minimize future friction and provide children with stability.

## Child support

Arizona determines child support obligations using statutory Child Support Guidelines that calculate financial responsibilities based on parental gross incomes, health insurance costs, daycare expenses, and the number of annual parenting days. We assist clients in establishing accurate initial support orders, adjusting existing orders when economic situations shift, and enforcing delinquent payments.

## Mediation

For parties seeking to avoid costly and protracted courtroom litigation, private mediation offers a collaborative forum to resolve disputes. In mediation, a neutral professional facilitates structured discussions to help couples reach voluntary, binding agreements regarding property division and parenting arrangements, preserving dignity and confidentiality.
