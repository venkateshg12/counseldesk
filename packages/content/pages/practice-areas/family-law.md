# Family Law in Arizona

Family problems are personal and stressful. You need calm legal help and practical solutions. Harbor & Vale Law helps you protect your children and your property.

## Divorce

We help couples through divorces where they agree (uncontested) and where they do not (contested). Arizona is a no-fault divorce state. That means you do not have to prove your spouse did something wrong to get divorced. We help you divide the property and debts you built up together, work out what counts as your own property, and ask for spousal maintenance, which is money one spouse pays the other after a divorce.

## Child custody

In Arizona, custody has two parts: legal decision-making, which is who makes the big choices about your child, and parenting time, which is when your child is with each parent. Judges decide based on what is best for the child. They look at how involved each parent is, how stable the home is and how close the bond is. We help you write a clear, workable parenting plan that cuts down on future arguments and gives your children stability.

## Child support

Arizona uses official Child Support Guidelines to work out who pays what. They look at each parent's income, health insurance costs, daycare costs and how many days a year the child lives with each parent. We help you set up a fair first order, change an existing order when money or life changes, and collect support that has not been paid.

## Mediation

Mediation is a way to settle things without a long and costly court fight. A neutral person leads the talks and helps you both reach an agreement on property and parenting. The agreement is binding, and the process stays private and respectful.
