# Arizona DUI Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding the procedural steps following an impaired driving allegation in Arizona can help you make informed decisions about your rights and driving privileges.

## After an arrest

Following an arrest for DUI in Arizona, law enforcement typically processes the driver, collects chemical breath or blood samples, and issues a citation along with an administrative suspension order. The officer may impound your motor vehicle under state mandatory immobilization rules. You will be scheduled for an initial arraignment in municipal or justice court to enter an initial plea.

## The MVD hearing deadline

In an Arizona DUI: you have 15 days after an MVD suspension notice to request a hearing. If you fail to file a timely hearing request within this strict 15-day window, your driving privilege will be automatically suspended. Requesting a hearing stays the suspension, allowing you to continue driving lawfully until the Motor Vehicle Division conducts the administrative proceeding.

## Ignition interlock

Arizona law mandates the installation of a certified ignition interlock device on all vehicles driven by individuals convicted of DUI offenses. The device requires drivers to submit an alcohol-free breath sample before starting the vehicle and at periodic intervals while driving. Compliance records are transmitted electronically to state regulatory authorities throughout the required installation period.
